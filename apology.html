The Pink Fairies

The pink fairies are the trail guides who assist the Furry Bodhis on their path. Do you know that they are pink fairy armadillos? pink fairy armadillos are the smallest species of armadillos and can be found in the deserts of central Argentina.

The Triple Gem Monastery

The Furry Bodhis must go on the adventure to reach the Triple Gem Monastery at the top of the mountain. They will look for the elusive grandmaster Bhante Pika. It is said that Bhante can bestow gems of unrivalled brilliance to every one who enters the monastery.
