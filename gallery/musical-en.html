The Story of Prince Siddhartha

🎀 1. Here comes the prince

Once upon a long long time, a Bodhisatta descended from Tusita Heaven to the foothills of the Himalaya. He wanted to be born in the Sakya kingdom and chose King Suddhodana and Queen Maya as his parents. The queen dreamt of a white elephant entering her body, and ten months later gave birth in Lumbini Garden. The baby prince immediately took seven steps, with lotus flowers appearing under his feet, and declared: "Chief am I in the world. This is my last birth. I will help all beings."

🧘 2. Asita the sage

An old sage named Asita heard of the prince's birth and hurried to the palace. He held the baby and saw thirty-two marks of greatness on his body. He smiled and then wept. The king and queen were worried and asked why? He replied that he smiled because the child would become a Buddha one day. But he wept because he would not live to see that day.

📛 3. Naming the Prince

On the fifth day, a naming ceremony was held. The king invited eight sages to choose a name for the prince. Seven held up two fingers and said, "The prince may become a great king or a Buddha." Only the youngest, Kondanna, held up one finger and said, "He will certainly become a Buddha!" The prince was named Siddhartha, meaning "wish fulfilled." On the seventh day, Queen Maya passed away. Because of the great merit of giving birth to a Buddha, she was reborn in heaven. Her sister, Prajapati, vowed to care for the prince as her own son.

🦢 4. The prince saves a swan

Prince Siddhartha was a very kind boy. One day, an injured swan fell before him. The swan was bleeding from an arrow wound. Then his cousin Devadatta ran over and said, "That's my swan! I shot it!" The prince wanted to save the swan and quarreled with Devadatta. They went to the council of elders for judgement. The elders said: "Life should belong to one who saves life and not one who seek to destroy it." The swan was given to Siddhartha. He cared for it until it healed, then set it free into the sky.
